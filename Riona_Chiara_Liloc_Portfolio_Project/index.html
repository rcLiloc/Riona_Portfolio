About Me

I'm a third-year Computer Engineering student at Mapúa Malayan Colleges Mindanao with a strong interest in computer hardware. While my course involves both hardware and software, I’ve always been more interested in the physical side of computers—building them, working with components, and understanding how everything works together behind the programs we use.

C++ is the programming language I’m most comfortable with because I like the level of control it provides and how closely it connects software with the way a computer operates.

Outside academics, I’m also a varsity basketball player. Balancing engineering and sports has become a big part of my college life, and when I have time to myself, I usually relax by listening to music.
